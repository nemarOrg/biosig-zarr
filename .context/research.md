# biosig-zarr Research Notes

Prior art and evidence that informed the specification.
Non-binding: an Architecture Decision Record in `decisions/` is the ruling.

## Prior art

- Open Microscopy Environment Next-Generation File Format (OME-NGFF) specification: the nearest neighbor, and the model for this repository's license split.
  Its RFC-9 (the zipped form of OME-Zarr) informed the `.bzx` packing rules and is prior art, not a dependency.
- Neurodata Without Borders (NWB) on Zarr, written by `hdmf-zarr`: considered and not adopted; the reasons are in NEMAR's Architecture Decision Record 0128, "The Zarr serving copy is not an NWB container".
